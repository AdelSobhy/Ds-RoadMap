### **📌 1. Kaggle ([kaggle.com](https://www.kaggle.com))**  
**✅ Best for:** Real-world datasets, competitions, community discussions  
- **Kaggle Datasets**: Find **thousands of datasets** for EDA & ML.  
- **Kaggle Notebooks**: See how others approach problems and share your own.  
- **Daily Challenges**: Participate in competitions and learn from top data scientists.  
- **Kaggle Courses**: Free mini-courses on Python, ML, NLP, SQL, etc.  

**🔥 Example Projects:**  
✔ Titanic survival prediction  
✔ House price prediction  
✔ Customer segmentation

### **📌 2. DataCamp ([datacamp.com](https://www.datacamp.com))**

- **Guided Projects**: Step-by-step mini-projects with real datasets.

## **🚀 Best Strategy for Daily Practice**
1. **Pick a platform** (Kaggle, StrataScratch, or DataCamp).  
2. **Set a daily goal** (1 dataset analysis, 1 SQL challenge, 1 ML experiment).  
3. **Track your progress** (GitHub, blog posts, LinkedIn).  
4. **Engage in discussions** (Reddit r/datascience, LinkedIn groups).  
5. **Apply knowledge in a portfolio project** every **2 weeks**.
